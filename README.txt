Lecture Notes Recorder

Open index.html in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

How to use:
1. Press Start Recording and allow microphone access.
2. Let the lecture play or speak near the microphone.
3. Press Stop when finished.
4. Press Make Notes to turn the transcript into editable notes.
5. Press Export PDF, then choose Save as PDF in the print window.

Tips:
- Live transcription depends on browser support. Chrome and Edge work best.
- If transcription is not available, paste a transcript into the Transcript box and press Make Notes.
- The audio recording can be downloaded after you stop recording.
